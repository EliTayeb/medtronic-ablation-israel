Build the CEO presentation, step by step

A workspace to assemble a decision-ready deck: grow sales with focused, proven marketing, and fix the costly ship-equipment-around-the-country process by owning gear where it makes sense — a playbook Israel proves and other similar markets can copy.

The objective

What this presentation sets out to do

A clear, decision-ready case for the leadership team.

📈 Grow sales

Increase revenue through a smart, focused, evidence-based marketing strategy — concentrate on the highest-yield accounts and franchises, lead with proven clinical value, and defend against competitive erosion.

🛠 Fix the process

Cut cost and service failures by buying selected equipment instead of shipping it around the country. Quantify the savings — and build it as a playbook that other similar markets can copy.

Step 2 · Analyze

Turn the data into the argument

📈 Marketing focus (grow sales)

  • A
    Rank accounts by procedure volume & consumable revenue; concentrate effort on the top tier.
  • B
    Segment by franchise (liver/kidney MWA vs bone RF) and tailor the value message to each.
  • C
    Lead with Emprint HP's proven edge (sphericity, margin, control rates) vs competitors.
  • D
    Defensive play vs cryoablation (Beilinson, Laniado) — workflow & anesthesia story.

🛠 Process case (buy vs ship)

  • A
    Total annual shipping cost across all shuttle sites.
  • B
    Delay → lost/postponed cases → revenue & service impact.
  • C
    Buy-vs-ship per candidate site: capital cost, annual saving, payback period.
  • D
    Parameterize everything so the model re-runs for the next country.

Strengths

Customer SWOT · Israel

Weaknesses

SWOT
  • No cryoablation option to counter Boston Scientific, so their cryo entry erodes our share.
  • We are concentrated entirely in heat ablation.
  • Shuttle logistics: we shuttle systems to centers with no unit on site, about ₪4,000 per transfer that we can save by placing a system recommendation
  • The Medtronic versus BSC share split is not yet quantified.

  • The first hands-on ablation classes in Israel.

Threats

SWOT
  • Cryoablation from Boston Scientific.
  • Cryoablation done by surgeons who prefer to keep cases in-house.
  • Lower DRG reimbursement compared with cryoablation.
  • War-related delays and cancellations of elective surgery.
  • BSC has built strong ties in the field.
  • Medtechnica is offering better pricing on parts and gas.

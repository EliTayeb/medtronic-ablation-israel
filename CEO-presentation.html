Ablation · Israel03
Grow sales

Smart, focused marketing — four moves

Concentrate effort where the yield is highest and the clinical story is strongest.

1 Target the top tier

Rank accounts by procedure volume & consumable revenue; put senior coverage on the centres that move the number.

2 Segment the message

Tailor the value story to each franchise — liver/kidney MWA vs. bone RF — instead of one generic pitch.

3 Lead with proof

Emprint HP's edge: 0.97 sphericity, >5 mm margins, 3.57% local progression at 12 mo — vs. Solero / Neuwave / Amica.

4 Defend vs. cryo

Win back Beilinson & Laniado with an anesthesia-light workflow story — the real reason cases are shifting.

Ablation · Israel06
The plan

Place systems where the math works

Phase placements by shipping intensity, procedure volume, and competitive risk.

Phase 1 · highest cost

Rambam · Shaare Zedek

Most shipments & delays per year — fastest payback on a placed unit.

Phase 2 · defend

Beilinson · Laniado

Cryo is taking cases — on-site availability removes the workflow excuse.

Phase 3 · on demand

Kaplan · HaEmek · Poria

Lower frequency — keep flexible shuttle or shared regional unit.

Also close quick wins: sign the Assaf Harofeh agreement, and clean the capital-asset register (recover lost/faulty units) to stop leaked service revenue.
